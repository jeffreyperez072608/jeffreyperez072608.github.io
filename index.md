# StickSquad

**A crew of stickmen who live on your desktop, get into trouble, and quietly
keep you on track.**

Up to five stickmen walk along your taskbar, climb the edges of your screen,
hang from the top, sit on your window title bars and use your desktop icons as
furniture. They play tag, have high-five contests, throw each other about, draw
themselves a ball or a set of monkey bars, and each has its own dance.

They also talk. Click one and it opens a little chat box. The AI runs **on your
own computer** — StickSquad ships with its own model, so there is nothing to
sign up for and nothing to pay monthly.

## What they can do

- **Talk and remember.** Each stickman has its own personality, its own voice,
  and its own memory of your conversations. You can read and delete everything
  it remembers.
- **Voice.** Talk to them out loud and they answer aloud, with their mouths
  moving. Works out of the box; better voices are an optional download.
- **Remind you.** "Remind me to drink water every 2 hours", "every weekday at
  9". They say it out loud when it is time.
- **Keep you company while you work**, and stay out of the way — they go quiet
  during Do Not Disturb and step aside for anything fullscreen.
- **Play.** Fights, tag, hide and seek, dance-offs with a disco ball. How much
  mischief they get up to is a setting, from "off" to "chaotic".
- **React to your Twitch chat** (optional). Viewers' messages appear over your
  stickmen, subs and raids get a celebration, and your chat can set them off
  with `!dance`. No Twitch login needed.
- **Do real work** (optional). Connect Google and they can draft and send
  email, read and add calendar events, and work with files you pick. Anything
  that sends or changes something asks you first.

## Private by default

- The AI runs locally. Your conversations do not leave your computer.
- **With nothing configured, StickSquad makes no network connections at all** —
  there is an automated test that fails the build if it ever does.
- No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no telemetry, no ads.
- Everything it stores is in your own user folder, and you can see and delete
  all of it from inside the app.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- About 8 GB of RAM (it will tell you if yours is tight, and offers a smaller
  model)
- A GPU helps but is not needed
